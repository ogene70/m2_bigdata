### ETL ELT

**ETL** or **ELT** define data flow in your data processing. One is not better than the other because their usage depends on your needs so it is your responsability to choose the correct process to use. All two defines  3 steps, 3 concerns that will be included in your **pipline**.

- Extract : define the step where you extract your data. Here you specify your datasources.
- Transform : define the step where you transform your data to harness them. It is the steps where you filter or clean the dataset.
- Load : define the step where you store your valuable data. It where you classify your metamorphosed data to be fully suitable for processing

Now you know more about data processing steps purposes. We will see now when to use those steps.
